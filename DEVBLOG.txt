You are Pants

Have you ever considered the lengths you might go as a pair of pants to be sold?

Now you have! Here is the Alpha update to our school project!

New Changes:
Fixed bug preventing player from turning mid jump.
Fixed bug preventing the level from ending
Combo timing for the player made easier
Level is given proper bounds to prevent player and enemy playing astronaut

Rusher Enemy created.
Moves towards the player and tries to jump on top of them.

Added a Giant blue cube that spawns when a player completes the second cube puzzle to help players who don't have a full grasp on the movement mechanics.

Puzzle cubes can be surfed.
Puzzle cubes do not exceed the player's max speed

Test Level Puzzle has had additional rooms/mechanics designed to guide the player.
Puzzle 1's room has been expanded to include a pit with a bottom to introduce the fact they will exist.
A Rusher has been placed in the Puzzle 1 room to introduce an easy to combat enemy.
Room 3 has been expanded to include a hallway with blockers, a turret, and spawners.
The turret is far behind several barricades to guide the player on how to avoid the projectiles.
The spawners are placed between two barricades forcing the player to jump into them in order to introduce them.
A UI element showcasing how many enemies need to be killed to complete the level and how many are currently dead.


Motivation:
The group's main motivation has been to make the test level have more juice, the enemies to feel more alive, and to introduce more polish like elements into the game.


What's Next:
Beginning development on a second level intending to showcase the air movement and puzzle cubes.
